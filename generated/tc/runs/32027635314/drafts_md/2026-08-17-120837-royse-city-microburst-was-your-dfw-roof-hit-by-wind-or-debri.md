## What Does Microburst Wind Damage Look Like on a Rockwall County Roof?

Microburst wind damage shows up as lifted, creased, or missing shingles, torn flashing, and displaced ridge caps. In the Royse City event, the National Weather Service measured winds up to 70 miles per hour — a straight-line downdraft that meteorologist Jennifer Dunn compared to lower-end tornadoes and larger straight-line wind events (Source: [NBC DFW](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/it-was-so-sudden-microburst-damages-royse-city-neighborhood-golf-course/4060610), August 2026). Wind uplift means the pressure differential across a roof edge that peels shingles back and breaks the seal strip beneath them. Because hail and wind damage is the leading cause of roof replacement in the Dallas area, even one microburst can push a thinned roof into a covered claim. Check your ridgeline, gutters, and the ground around your home for shingle fragments after any high-wind event.

## How Do I Tell Debris Damage From Hail Damage After the Storm?

Debris damage leaves irregular gouges, punctures, and impact marks, while hail leaves round, soft bruises that expose the black asphalt mat. The Royse City microburst sent metal sheets from a torn equipment barn roof onto neighboring homes — a debris hazard distinct from hail (Source: [NBC DFW](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/it-was-so-sudden-microburst-damages-royse-city-neighborhood-golf-course/4060610), August 2026). Granule loss means the protective mineral coating shedding off shingles into gutters — a handful is normal weathering, but a pile signals impact. Hail strikes leave round depressions that tend to fail within one to five years, so documenting them early matters for coverage (Source: [tcroofingexperts.com](https://tcroofingexperts.com/blog/p.260810000/what-happens-between-a-dallas-fort-worth-hail-inspection-and-your-insurance), August 2026). Photograph any dented soft metals — vents, AC fins, and flashing — because those are the clearest tie-breakers between wind, debris, and hail.
