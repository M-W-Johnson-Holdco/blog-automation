### What counts as functional roof damage versus normal aging?

Functional damage includes cracked or lifted shingles, bald granule-loss spots, failed flashing, and active leaks — issues that compromise the roof's job. Normal aging is gradual surface wear that hasn't yet breached the system's protection. Because the line can blur, documentation matters. A dated inspection report that labels each finding helps both your insurer and you understand what actually needs attention.
