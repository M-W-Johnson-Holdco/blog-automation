## What are the real signs of hail damage on your Dallas roof?

The clearest signs of hail damage are granule loss in gutters, soft round bruises on shingles, and dented soft metals. After a hail event, asphalt shingles shed granules into gutters and downspouts — a handful is normal weathering, but a pile signals impact (Source: [bertroofing.com](https://bertroofing.com/services/storm-damage), August 2026). Hail strikes leave round depressions that expose the black asphalt mat, and those bruises tend to fail within one to five years. In older Richardson and Garland neighborhoods where 1980s and 1990s roofs have already weathered multiple hail seasons, thinned granule coverage means a fresh hail event worsens fast. Check gutters, downspouts, vents, and AC fins — if those soft metals are dented, your roof took hits too.

## FAQ

### Does a hailstorm's forecast risk level determine whether my claim is covered?
No — coverage depends on your policy terms and whether the damage is storm-caused rather than pre-existing wear, not on the storm's risk category. Insurers evaluate the actual damage, not the forecast label. A lower-category storm carrying hail and gusty winds can still damage a worn Tarrant County roof. The most reliable way to prove new damage is a dated inspection completed before the storm hits.

### Why is a roofer asking me to waive my deductible a red flag in Texas?
Waiving a deductible is illegal in Texas, so any roofer offering it is signaling they operate outside the rules. Legitimate contractors document your damage and work directly with your adjuster without gimmicks. Storm chasers who canvass door-to-door after events often lead with deductible offers to close fast. Ask how long a company has served the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex and how they document damage before signing anything.

### How can I tell if the granules in my Garland gutters mean real hail damage?

Asphalt shingles shed their granule layer when hail strikes, exposing the black asphalt mat underneath. In older Garland neighborhoods with 1980s and 1990s roofs, granule coverage is already thinned, so a hail event can accelerate failure. Have the roof photographed from above to confirm whether the loss is storm-caused.

### What does RCV mean on my roof insurance claim?
RCV means replacement cost value — what it costs to replace your roof with like materials at today's prices. Understanding RCV helps you evaluate whether an offered settlement actually covers a full replacement. ### How do I document roof damage for insurance in Dallas County?
Capture dated photographs of granule loss in gutters, bruised shingles, and any dented soft metals like vents, downspouts, and AC fins. Ground-level photos miss most shingle bruising, so aerial imagery from a drone inspection is far more useful to an adjuster. In Dallas County's mixed housing stock, clear before-and-after evidence separates storm damage from age. Keep this documentation on file even in years you do not file a claim.

### Are lifted shingles on my Arlington roof a covered problem?
Lifted shingles caused by wind can qualify as storm damage, because they reseal imperfectly and become long-term failure points. Wind speeds above 60 mph can lift shingles even on a structurally sound roof, which is common in Arlington's exposed suburban lots. Whether it is covered depends on your policy and on proving the wind event caused it. Have a contractor inspect the reseal points and document them promptly.
