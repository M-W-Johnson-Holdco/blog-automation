## What Should You Do Immediately After a Tree Falls Through Your Roof?

Get everyone out of the house first, then call 911 if anyone is injured. In the Kennesaw incident, one person received minor injuries when the dying tree crashed through the home (Source: [WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/kennesaw-family-loses-home-after-tree-falls-through-roof-during-october-storm/JHVH4ZZCSJBMLO5ED3LACDJ3TI), November 2026). A tree that weighs 15 tons can compromise load-bearing walls and ceilings, so treat the structure as unsafe until a professional clears it. Shut off electricity to affected rooms if you can do so safely, since punctured roofs often tear through wiring. Once everyone is safe, photograph the damage from the ground, cover exposed openings with a tarp to limit water intrusion, and begin your insurance claim — in that order.

## How Do You Know If a Tree Near Your Roof Is a Threat?

A dying or dead tree leaning toward your home is the single clearest warning sign — the Kennesaw tree that destroyed the home was already dying before it fell (Source: [WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/video/local-video/kennesaw-family-loses-home-after-tree-falls-through-roof-during-october-storm/4da75c2a-c324-490d-a3a6-e2c79a0cbfbb), November 2026). Watch for bare branches when nearby trees have leaves, fungus or mushrooms at the base, hollow or cracked trunks, and soil heaving on one side of the root plate. Metro Atlanta's clay-heavy soils hold water poorly after heavy rain, which loosens root anchorage and lets tall hardwoods topple even in moderate wind. If any limb or trunk could reach your roofline when it falls, have an arborist assess the tree and a roofer assess the roof's vulnerability underneath it.

## Which Cobb and Metro Atlanta Neighborhoods Face the Highest Tree-Fall Risk?

Older, heavily wooded suburbs with mature tree canopies carry the greatest tree-through-roof risk. Kennesaw and Marietta neighborhoods built out in the 1980s and 1990s often sit under 40- to 70-foot hardwoods that have reached the end of their healthy lifespan, and a single dying oak there can weigh tons — exactly the scenario WSB-TV documented. East Cobb's established subdivisions pair aging two-story homes with dense canopy, so a falling trunk strikes a larger roof surface. In Roswell and Woodstock, sloped lots and Cherokee County's red-clay subsoil reduce root grip after saturating rains, raising the odds of a lean becoming a collapse. Sandy Springs and Dunwoody properties with tall pines face a similar threat, since pines snap rather than bend in gusts. Across Fulton and DeKalb, the common thread is the same: a mature tree in decline positioned within striking distance of an older roof deck.

## How Does a Tree Strike Turn Into a Roof Replacement Claim?

A tree strike almost always forces a full roof replacement rather than a patch, because the impact fractures the roof deck, snaps trusses, and splits shingles far beyond the entry point. Water driven in during the same storm then soaks insulation and drywall, so hidden damage extends beyond what you see from the yard. Insurance claims for tree-through-roof events hinge on documentation — photos of the tree, the entry hole, interior water damage, and a contractor's structural assessment.
