## What is the difference between RCV and ACV on my policy?

RCV means replacement cost value and ACV means actual cash value — the difference decides how much of your roof replacement the insurer actually pays. On an RCV policy, recoverable depreciation is released only against the actual completed work and final invoice; on an ACV policy the check is yours, but it rarely covers a full replacement in the first place (Source: [domroofing.com](https://www.domroofing.com/roof-academy/roof-insurance-claim-hail-storm-georgia), July 2026). Metro Atlanta homeowners in older Decatur and Tucker neighborhoods, where roofs may be a decade or more into their service life, often find their policies have quietly shifted toward ACV as roofs age. The takeaway: read whether your policy pays RCV or ACV before the next storm, because it determines your out-of-pocket cost on a claim.
