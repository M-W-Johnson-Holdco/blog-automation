## What Did ARMA Actually Dispute in the IBHS Wind Report?

ARMA concluded that the IBHS report's broad conclusions were compromised by testing-method problems, not that asphalt shingles perform poorly in wind. The trade group said IBHS evaluated 14 products across seven manufacturers but shifted testing procedures mid-project, used unrepresentative grab-sampling, and omitted positive performance outcomes along with enhanced manufacturer installation guidelines (Source: [nrca.net](https://www.nrca.net/RoofingNews/arma-responds-to-ibhs-report-regarding-wind-performance-of-asphalt-shingles.10-27-2026.13573/Details/Story), October 2026). For Metro Atlanta homeowners, the takeaway is practical: a laboratory debate over testing protocols is less useful than knowing whether your own shingles were nailed, sealed, and installed correctly. The actionable step is to ask any contractor how they verify installation against the manufacturer's published wind-rating requirements.

## How Does Shingle Age and Installation Affect Wind Resistance in Atlanta?

Aging shingles and shortcut installations are the two factors most likely to undermine wind performance — far more than the manufacturing quality ARMA defended. Asphalt shingles rely on a heat-activated sealant strip that bonds tabs together; as shingles age and granules shed, that bond weakens, and under-driven or over-driven nails leave tabs free to flutter in high wind. In Metro Atlanta's postwar frame homes around Decatur and Brookhaven, and in the dense 1980s townhome stock across East Cobb, deferred maintenance compounds the problem because minor lifted edges go unnoticed until a storm peels them back. This is precisely the gap ARMA flagged — real-world durability depends on installation guidelines the disputed study downplayed. Have a contractor check seal integrity, nail patterns, and flashing at every penetration before deciding whether to repair or replace.

A documented inspection of seal integrity, nailing, and flashing gives you a reliable answer for your specific roof.
