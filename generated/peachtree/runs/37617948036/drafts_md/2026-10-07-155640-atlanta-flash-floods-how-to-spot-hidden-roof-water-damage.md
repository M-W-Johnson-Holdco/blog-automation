## What should Metro Atlanta homeowners check after flash flooding?

Homeowners should check the attic, ceilings, gutters, and roof penetrations after flash flooding and gusty storms. Start in the attic with a flashlight — look for damp insulation, water trails on rafters, or daylight showing through the decking, all signs that water found a path in. Inspect ceilings and upper walls for fresh stains or bubbling paint, which often surface days after the rain. Outside, clear gutters of granules and debris and look for lifted or missing shingles, since the 40 mph gusts reported in Franklin and Hart counties are enough to break seal strips. Flashing around chimneys, skylights, and vents is the most common hidden-leak entry point, so note any gaps or rust and bring them to a licensed and insured roofing contractor.
