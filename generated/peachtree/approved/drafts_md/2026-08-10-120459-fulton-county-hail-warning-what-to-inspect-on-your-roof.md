## What Hail Damage Actually Looks Like on an Atlanta Roof?

Hail damage on asphalt shingles shows up as round bruises, cracked mats, and bare spots where the protective granules have been knocked away. Those granules shield the asphalt from UV rays, so once they're gone, the shingle ages faster and becomes prone to leaks. The NWS forecast specifically called out large hail for Fulton County this evening, which is exactly the size range that dents softer metals and fractures aging shingles (Source: [NWS Atlanta](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?zoneid=OHZ002), August 2026). Check your gutters and downspout splash zones first — a sudden pile of granule grit is one of the clearest early signs. If you find it, photograph the debris before it washes away.

## What Should Fulton County Homeowners Inspect First After Tonight's Storm?

Start from the ground and inside the house, then move to a professional check for the roof itself. Walk the perimeter looking for dented gutters, torn screens, cracked window trim, and split fascia and soffit — collateral hail hits often predict roof damage above. Inside, scan ceilings and the top corners of upstairs rooms for fresh water stains, which spread fast once a shingle's protective layer fails. Check metal roof vents, chimney caps, and flashing for dimpling, since bruised flashing is a frequent entry point for water. Do not climb the roof yourself — wet, hail-battered shingles are slick and unstable.

## How Does a Damaged Roof Turn Into a Denied Insurance Claim?

Insurers can deny hail claims when they attribute damage to pre-existing wear or deferred maintenance rather than the storm. That's why documentation timing matters: cracked flashing, missing granules, or lifted shingles that predate tonight weaken your case, while fresh, dated photos tied to a confirmed NWS hail event support it. Failed flashing around chimneys and vents is a common denial trigger, because a small unaddressed gap can be blamed on age instead of impact. A professional roof inspection distinguishes new hail bruising from old wear and gives the adjuster a clear, defensible record.
